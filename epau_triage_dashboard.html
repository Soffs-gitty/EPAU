EPAU Triage Prioritisation Guide

High Risk (Immediate MO Review)

  • Heavy PV bleeding (soaking pads) or passage of products of conception
  • Hemodynamic instability (hypotension, tachycardia, syncope/dizziness)
  • Severe unilateral iliac fossa pain / suspected ruptured ectopic pregnancy

Medium Risk (Priority Review)

  • Moderate PV bleeding with stable vital signs
  • Hyperemesis Gravidarum requiring IV hydration / antiemetics
  • Mild-to-moderate cramping with spotting in early pregnancy

Low Risk (Standard Queue)

  • Minimal brown discharge or mild spotting without pain
  • Routine viability / dating ultrasound referral
  • Follow-up review for stable missed miscarriage / serial β-hCG
